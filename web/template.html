    Before you book

    • Links open each airline's award search for that route and date, set to 1 adult. Some start in economy. Switch to __N__ travelers and the right cabin; that also confirms __N__ seats are open.
    • Search before you transfer points. Transfers can't be undone, and this is cached data.
    • American doesn't report seat counts. Check aa.com for __N__ open seats. More than 60 days out it may show only one Iberia business seat; the same flight can have more on iberia.com or britishairways.com.
    • Etihad Guest books Air France flights by phone only.
    • Positioning flights are separate tickets. Fly to the gateway the day before.
    • Refresh on seats.aero opens that route and date on seats.aero. Sign in with your Pro account there to run a live search, since this page is a snapshot.
    • Still to come: airlines release seats roughly 330–360 days ahead, so the latest dates fill in day by day.
